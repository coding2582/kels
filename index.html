04 Our story

A long road to the
corner of Hewitt.

"Over a year to get here. Worth every bit of it."

Sam Dahl always wanted a cafe of his own. It took well over a year to make it happen at 1909 Hewitt Avenue, right in the middle of downtown Everett, a short walk from Angel of the Winds Arena.

Permits, health-department backlogs, more phone calls than he'd like to count. On June 18, 2025, Sam and his mom cut the ribbon together. A year on, the regulars are steady, the menu is consistent, and a bar is taking shape downstairs.

Made to Order

Espresso pulled to order, pastries baked each morning.

Rooted Downtown

Locally owned, built specifically for downtown Everett.

Family Run

Sam and his mom opened this together. That shows in how it runs.
